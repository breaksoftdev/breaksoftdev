### 🕹️ **Scenic AR**
- **Description**: An augmented reality application that empowers users to build and customize 3D scenes with personalized avatars, bringing imagination to life in real-world settings.
- **Key Features**: Intuitive scene editing, avatar customization, and seamless AR overlay.

### 🌍 **AR Geolocation Game**
- **Description**: A location-based AR game where virtual objects dynamically spawn and interact based on real-world GPS coordinates, encouraging exploration and adventure.
- **Key Features**: GPS-triggered events, dynamic object placement, and multiplayer elements for shared experiences.
- **Technologies**: Unity, AR Foundation, GPS APIs for precise geolocation.

### 🎲 **Multiplayer Strategy Game**
- **Description**: A real-time multiplayer strategy game with procedurally generated environments and AI-driven decision-making systems for strategic depth.
- **Key Features**: Turn-based and real-time modes, procedural terrain generation, and intelligent NPC behaviors.
- **Technologies**: Unity, Photon Networking, procedural algorithms.

### 🏗️ **AI-Powered Virtual City Simulation**
- **Description**: A 2D simulation of urban traffic management with AI-controlled vehicles, pathfinding, and real-time collision avoidance.
- **Key Features**: Dynamic traffic flow, AI decision engines, and simulation analytics.
- **Technologies**: Unity, A* pathfinding, AI frameworks for behavior modeling.

### 🎥 **Unity Video Call App (Agora SDK)**
- **Description**: A high-performance video calling application supporting side-by-side (SBS) streaming for low-latency 4K video communication between Android devices.
- **Key Features**: Ultra-low latency, high-resolution streaming, and cross-device compatibility.
- **Technologies**: Unity, Agora SDK, Android-specific optimizations.
